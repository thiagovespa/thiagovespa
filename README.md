### Hi, I'm Thiago, but everyone calls me **Vespa** 🛵

**Technology executive, researcher and educator.** I help engineering teams grow without losing quality, and I've spent over 20 years combining software architecture, people leadership and research.

- 🎓 **PhD in Computer Science** (University of São Paulo), researching AI and similarity search, and **Doctor of Leadership** (Andrews University, USA)
- 📘 Author of [*Modelos de Decisão para Empresas de Software*](https://thiagovespa.com.br/livros/modelos-de-decisao/) (Decision Models for Software Companies)
- 📄 Published in IEEE, Elsevier and Wiley journals
- 🧠 Curious about almost everything: logic, astronomy, archaeology, ham radio and quantum mechanics

#### 💼 Experience

- **CTO** · 2023 – present<br>
  Technology strategy, architecture and engineering leadership; built a tech organization from scratch and worked on one of Latin America's largest AI agent deployments for the public sector.
- **Head of Innovation and mentor** at a space-tech startup · 2024 – present
- **Head of Development and Technology** · 2008 – 2023<br>
  Started as a software architect delivering distributed systems for Cielo, Deutsche Bank, Walmart and Rede Globo, then scaled engineering from 3 to 150 people with a Tech Manager / Tech Lead model and a leadership program.
- **Educator** · 2007 – present<br>
  Computer Science lecturer (AI, compilers, formal languages), corporate trainer and executive director of a tech training company.
